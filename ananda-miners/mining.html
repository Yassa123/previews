Eco-friendly mining

We believe eco-friendly mining is the future.

Proper management of mined water is central to how we work. At our mines, waste water is channelled through a series of settling pits so that mud and silt are deposited before the water is released into the river system.

  • Several settling pits in series to drop mud and silt
  • Natural, biodegradable filter materials
  • Cleaner water released back to the river system
  • Advice for customers who want to do the same
